Heartless

9pm. Don't be late.

Earl and Flynn have been meaning to check out the abandoned house. The window they climb through turns into a wall behind them. What lies ahead is a hallway that runs on longer than any building should, lit by fluorescent tubes, with an intercom that never quite says anything.

Heartless is a short horror film about guilt and what follows you home. It starts, as many nights do, with a text thread.
